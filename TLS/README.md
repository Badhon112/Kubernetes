# TLS (Transport layer Security)

- _Cryptography_
  - Cryptography is the art of encrypting information in a way that unintended recipients can't understand.
  - In Cryptography, an original human-readable message, known as plaintext, is transformed by an algorithm, or series of mathematical operations, into unreadable text called ciphertext
  - Ciphertext is very hard to decode
  - The prefix crypt means hidden and suffix graphy means writing

- _Encryption vs Cryptography_
  - _Cryptography_ : is study of methods like encryption, it's main objective is to provide methods to secure and protect information and communications using encryption and related techniques.
  - _Encryption_ : is the way (process) to encrypt and decrypt data. It is considered as principal application of cryptography
